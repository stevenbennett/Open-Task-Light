**Parts required for this section:**

* Lighting sub-assembly
* Electronics housing sub-assembly
* LED
* Control Board
* Power track (1x)
* Horizontal rail (the one with the heat pipe installed)

**Tools required for this section:**

* 1.3 mm hex driver
* 1.5 mm hex driver
* 2.5 mm hex driver
* 3 mm hex driver
