# Assembling the counterweight

### Gather the following

* **`Counterweight`** parts bag
* **Weight material** (about 500 g remaining from the single 1,650 g bag included with current freestanding kits, or the 550 g bag included with clamping kits)
* Printed parts
  * [Counterweight Enclosure](/open-task-light/printing-guide/counterweight/counterweight-enclosure.md)
  * [Counterweight Lid](/open-task-light/printing-guide/counterweight/counterweight-lid.md)
  * [Counterweight Belt Clip](/open-task-light/printing-guide/counterweight/counterweight-belt-clip.md)
  * [Counterweight Funnel](/open-task-light/printing-guide/tools/counterweight-funnel.md)

Tools:

* **1.3 mm hex driver**
* **2.5 mm hex driver**

### Add weight material to the counterweight enclosure

This step can get messy if you're not careful so please print the funnel tool and insert in the top of the counterweight enclosure.

If you have the clamping base kit you can also secure the c-clamp to the bottom of the counterweight enclosure to prevent it from tipping over as your pouring in the weight material.

<figure>
<img src="/images/fd86c679dac2.jpg" alt="Counterweight enclosure standing upright with the funnel in its open top and the bag of steel shot beside it">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="37,24" label="60,14" match="funnel" link="Print guide: printing-guide/tools/counterweight-funnel">Counterweight Funnel <note>Push it into the open top of the upright enclosure before pouring. Clamping kit owners can hold the enclosure down with the c-clamp so it doesn't tip.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Add about 400 g of material to the counterweight enclosure. You don't have to get it perfect because we'll micro-adjust later on.

If you have a scale handy at this stage you can weigh your assembled horizontal arm at this stage. Remember that you'll also have to add the weight of the assembled carriage and subtract the weight of the counterweight enclosure (about 100g).

### Secure the counterweight lid

Remove the funnel and insert the counterweight lid.

<figure><img src="/images/1a29382f4e58.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Use the M2x6 mm flathead screw to secure the lid in place. Finger tighten.

<figure>
<img src="/images/4e7e6dda3c87.jpg" alt="Driving the M2x6 mm flathead screw into the counterweight lid with a 1.3 mm hex driver">

Finger tight. Not the longer M3x10 mm screws in the same bag; those are for the flex tabs.</note></li>

### Install the counterweight flex tabs

With the lid secure, lay the counterweight with the belt slot facing up.

<figure>
<img src="/images/15bdb185500b.jpg" alt="Counterweight lying flat with the belt slot and the two flex tab pockets facing up">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50.5,14" label="66,12">Belt slot <note>The ribbed slot at one end, between the two countersunk holes. This face goes up; the belt clip goes here later.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
#### Insert the springs

Insert the two springs in their corresponding holes (the springs are identical).

<figure>
<img src="/images/c4e62c056131.jpg" alt="Counterweight lying flat with the belt slot facing up and a spring seated in each flex tab pocket">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50.3,25.5" label="66,26" qty="2" match="springs" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">Counterweight Flex Tab Spring <note>One drops into the hole in each flex tab pocket, here and at the far end. Both springs are identical.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
#### Install the flex tabs

Insert the top flex tab with the flat face down.

Use a M3x10 mm screw to secure it in place. Finger tighten.

<figure>
<img src="/images/828cb3451905.jpg" alt="Hands driving the M3x10 mm screw into the top flex tab with a 2.5 mm hex driver">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50.5,43.5" label="30,32" qty="2" match="top flex tab" link="Part page: self-sourcing-guide/additional-printed-parts/counterweight-flex-tab">Counterweight Flex Tab <note>Flat face down, over the spring; top tab first, then the lower one. The tab should sit proud and squash down when pressed.</note></li>
<li dot="51.3,49.3" label="30,58" qty="1 per tab" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M3x10 mm screw <note>Driven with the 2.5 mm hex driver. Finger tighten only; overtightening stops the tab from flexing.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Repeat for the lower flex tab.

### Attach the belt

Locate your belt and counterweight belt clip.

<figure>
<img src="/images/266aa3e2bf04.jpg" alt="Coiled belt on the table and the small counterweight belt clip held up between two fingers">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="23,58" label="30,38" link="Part prep: self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/gt2-timing-belt">Belt <note>The long toothed belt from the Counterweight parts bag. One end goes into the clip on this page; the other end reaches the carriage later.</note></li>
<li dot="51.7,46" label="36,30" link="Print guide: printing-guide/counterweight/counterweight-belt-clip">Counterweight Belt Clip <note>The small printed part with two countersunk holes and a belt slot through the middle.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Hold the belt clip as shown with the belt slot towards the bottom. Insert the belt through the side of the clip with the countersunk holes, with the belt teeth facing down.

<figure>
<img src="/images/6b93631748dc.jpg" alt="Belt clip held upright between two fingers with the belt being fed in from the side with the countersunk holes, teeth facing down">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49.5,48.4" label="36,24" match="belt teeth facing down">Belt <note>The toothed side of the belt points down as it goes into the clip. Wrong way up and the teeth won't grip the counterweight.</note></li>
<li dot="52.8,40.6" label="62,24">Countersunk holes <note>Feed the belt in from this side of the clip, the side with the two countersunk screw holes.</note></li>
<li dot="53.2,47.5" label="52,84" match="belt slot">Belt clip slot <note>The through-slot in the clip that the belt passes. Hold the clip so the slot sits towards the bottom.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Insert about 8 mm of belt through the slot. When you fold the belt up, it should sit within the recessed area of the belt clip as shown.

<figure>
<img src="/images/8821272366dc.jpg" alt="Belt clip held upright with about 8 mm of belt pushed through the slot and folded up into the recess on the clip face">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49.5,44.5" label="66,70">Recessed area <note>The shallow pocket on the clip face between the two holes. About 8 mm of belt goes through the slot; the folded end should sit flat inside the recess, not proud of the face.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Insert the belt clip and belt combo into the counterweight (with the belt slot closest to the flex tab).

<figure>
<img src="/images/49deccf6622d.jpg" alt="Belt clip with the belt attached being pushed into the end of the counterweight next to the top flex tab">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52,55" label="38,44" match="belt clip and belt combo" link="Print guide: printing-guide/counterweight/counterweight-belt-clip">Counterweight Belt Clip <note>Goes into the pocket at the end of the counterweight, belt slot end towards the white flex tab. The belt should exit right beside the tab.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Secure the belt clip using two M2x6 mm flat head screws.

<figure>
<img src="/images/a9b9c8208434.jpg" alt="Driving one of the two M2x6 mm flat head screws into the belt clip at the end of the counterweight, next to the white flex tab">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52.2,49.6" label="30,36" qty="2" match="M2x6 mm flat head screws" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M2x6 mm flat head screw <note>1.3 mm hex driver. One at each end of the belt clip, finger tight. The clip sits flush in its pocket once both are in.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/f0fdc7d43088.jpg" alt="Close-up of the end of the counterweight with the belt clip screwed in beside the white flex tab and the belt running out of the slot"></figure>

Give the belt a firm tug to ensure it's secure.

<figure><img src="/images/b275a4eb42b0.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Your counterweight is assembled!
