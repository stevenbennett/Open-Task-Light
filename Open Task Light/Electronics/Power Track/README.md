# Power Track PCB

The Power Track is the long, two-conductor PCB that carries 24 V along the Open Task Light rails.

For fabrication details, required parts, and assembly instructions, see the [Power Track section of the Open Task Light assembly guide](https://open-task-light.gitbook.io/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/custom-manufactured-parts/power-track).

## Files

- `power-track-gerbers.zip` contains the fabrication and drill files required to order the bare PCB.
- `Source/Power Track.brd` and `Source/Power Track.sch` are the editable Autodesk Fusion Electronics source files.
- `Assembly/power-track-bom.csv` is the Fusion Electronics BOM for the two parts installed on the PCB.
- Pick-and-place files are not included because the Power Track is intended to be assembled by hand.

## Manufacturing notes

Upload the Gerber ZIP when ordering the bare PCB. Assemble the surface-mount standoff and right-angle Micro-Lock connector by hand as described in the assembly guide.

Confirm the board outline, plated and non-plated holes, and exposed contact areas in the manufacturer's preview before ordering.
