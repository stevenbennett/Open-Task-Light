# LED Cable Assembly

This guide will walk your through preparation of the cable that connects the LED holder to the control board.

### Gather the following

* 100 mm or 150 mm Micro-lock cable

**Tools:**

* Wire cutters or scissors
* Wire strippers
* Soldering iron
* Solder

I use a Sharpie like this to mark the wire:

<figure><img src="/images/f477e33c8fa0.jpg" alt=""></figure>

***

#### Prepare the wires

Cut the Micro-Lock cable to 75 mm, measuring from where the wires exit the Micro-Lock connector housing to the end of the wire.

You can clean any sticker residue from the cable using isopropyl alcohol or Goo Gone.

Strip the wire ends so **6 mm** of wire is exposed.

Twist the bare wires together and apply solder to tin them. This will make it possible to insert the bare wire into the LED holder during assembly.

#### Mark the positive wire

Locate the cable's positive wire. When looking down at the cable with the cable clip facing you, the positive wire will be on the **left**:

<figure>
<img src="/images/df630b8eabd4.jpg" alt="Micro-Lock connector held with its cable clip facing the camera, a tool tip resting on the left-hand wire">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="37,29" label="22,12" match="positive wire">Positive wire <note>With the clip facing you, the wire on the left is positive. Mark this one.</note></li>

Mark the positive wire near the bare wire end.

You're done!
