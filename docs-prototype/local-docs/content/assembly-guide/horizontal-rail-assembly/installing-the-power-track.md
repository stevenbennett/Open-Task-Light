# Installing the power track

### Gather the following

* **Electronics housing** (from previous step)
* **Horizontal rail** (prepared in previous steps)
* **Power track** (1x)
* Printed parts
  * [Power Track Support](/open-task-light/printing-guide/rail-attachments/power-track-support.md)(2x)
  * [End Cap](/open-task-light/printing-guide/rail-attachments/end-cap.md)(1x)
* From the **`Electronics Housing`** parts bag
  * **M5x8 mm screw** (1x)

Tools:

* **1.5 mm hex driver**
* **2.5 mm hex driver**

### Insert the power track

Insert about 5cm of the connector side of the power track into the side channel of the horizontal arm (the one with the cable sticking out of the electronics housing).

<figure>
<img src="/images/7de7248ddec2.jpg" alt="The connector end of the power track being slid a few centimetres into the side channel of the horizontal rail, with the electronics housing at the far end">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="62,52" label="75,40" match="connector side">Power track <note>The connector end goes in first. Push it in only about 5 cm for now.</note></li>
<li dot="56,48" label="45,28">Side channel <note>The slot along the side of the rail that has the housing cable sticking out of it, not the other side.</note></li>
<li dot="6,12" label="16,36">Electronics housing <note>Its cable comes out of the same side channel the track goes into.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
### Insert power track supports

Insert one of the power track supports between the power track and rail interior with the smoother, printbed side of the spacer touching the back of the PCB.

<figure>
<img src="/images/796108e713cb.jpg" alt="A power track support being slid into the rail channel between the power track and the rail wall, with the second support on the table">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49,43" label="38,22" qty="1 of 2" match="power track supports" link="Print guide: printing-guide/rail-attachments/power-track-support">Power track support <note>Slides in between the PCB and the rail interior. Smooth side against the back of the PCB. The second one goes in the same way after the track is pushed a little further in.</note></li>
<li dot="62,32" label="52,8">Power track <note>The connector end is already about 5 cm into the side channel.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Slide the power track support in completely.

<figure><img src="/images/ee6cd38270ec.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Slide the power track into the rail a bit further and insert the second power track support the same way.

Push the spacers in as far as they'll go, but don't push the power track all the way in yet.

<figure>
<img src="/images/bf8a12a9dfc2.jpg" alt="Rail with both supports pushed in and the power track still sticking out of the end, with the end cap and screw waiting on the table">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="56,40" label="63,14">Power track <note>Leave it sticking out like this for now. It gets pushed fully home after the housing is plugged in.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
### Connect the power track to the electronics housing

On the opposite end of the rail, plug the electronics enclosure into the power track.

### Secure the power track

<figure><img src="/images/a86e34c46306.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/ecf58d9bfe8a.jpg" alt="End-on view of the rail showing the power track sitting in the side channel with its set screw block at the end"></figure>
Attach the end cap to the end of the rail using an M5x8 mm screw. Hand tighten.

<figure>
<img src="/images/a705c17b7278.jpg" alt="The end cap held against the end of the rail while an M5x8 screw is threaded in with a hex driver">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="43,51" label="43,72" qty="1" link="Print guide: printing-guide/rail-attachments/end-cap">End cap <note>Sits over the end of the rail and covers the end of the power track.</note></li>
<li dot="46,51" label="54,26" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5x8 mm screw <note>Hand tighten only, with the 2.5 mm hex driver.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Use a 1.5 mm hex driver to tighten the power track set screw and secure it in place.

<figure>
<img src="/images/5b388f6bf6f1.jpg" alt="Tightening the power track set screw at the end of the rail with a 1.5 mm hex driver">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="53,52" label="44,86" match="set screw" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M3 set screw <note>The grub screw in the black block at the end of the track. Snug is enough.</note></li>
<li dot="56,50" label="64,22" qty="Tool">1.5 mm hex driver <note>Small driver. Don't confuse it with the 2.5 mm one used for the end cap.</note></li>
<li dot="30,41" label="38,18">Power track <note>Now fully inside the rail, held by the end cap and the set screw.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/7f5050fb0c27.jpg" alt=""></figure>

### Tighten the electronics housing screws

Return to the four M5 screws in the electronics housing. Finger tighten them.

<figure><img src="/images/7b70c1711167.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/19e8db4b24f5.jpg" alt=""></figure>

You're done with the horizontal rail! This is the toughest (and definitely messiest) portion of the assembly. Set the rail aside for now, it's smooth sailing from here 🙂
