# Open Task Light Electronics

Design source, bills of materials, and manufacturing files for the three custom PCBs used in the Open Task Light.

## Available files

- [Control Board](Control%20Board/) - Fusion source, Gerber and drill files, a component BOM, and pick-and-place exports for the main electronics PCB.
- [Power Link](Power%20Link/) - Fusion source, Gerber and drill files, a component BOM, and pick-and-place exports for the carriage power connection.
- [Power Track](Power%20Track/) - Fusion source, Gerber and drill files, and a component BOM for the rail power conductors.

For fabrication guidance and assembly instructions, see the [Custom Manufactured Parts section of the Open Task Light assembly guide](https://open-task-light.gitbook.io/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/custom-manufactured-parts). Check the guide before ordering boards or assembling the electronics.
