### Orientation

Flat surface on buildplate.

<figure><img src="/images/471edf385847.png" alt=""></figure>

Optionally, paint a seam line on the back of the shade cylinder.

<figure>
<img src="/images/d12c7b38e49b.png" alt="Slicer view of the shade standing on its flat face with a seam line painted as a blue stripe on the back of the cylinder">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="62,40" label="82,18">Seam line <note>Painted seam. Keeps the layer seam in one tidy line on the side that faces the housing instead of scattering it around the visible cylinder.</note></li>
<li dot="50,45" label="15,25">Back of the shade cylinder <note>The side with the mounting tabs. It sits against the electronics housing, so this is where a seam is least visible.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
