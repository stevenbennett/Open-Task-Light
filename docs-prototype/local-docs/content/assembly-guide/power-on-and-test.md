# Power-On & Test

Before you start using the light, give it one careful inspection and make sure it stays powered throughout its full range of motion.

### 1. Inspect the assembly

Before connecting power:

* Move the carriage and counterweight through their full range of motion. They should move freely.
* Wiggle the carriage and check the wheels for rattle or play. They should sit firmly against the rail without making the carriage difficult to move.
* Make sure you're using the 24 V DC power supply that came with the kit.
* Look for loose connections or pinched, exposed, or damaged wires.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Do not connect power if any wiring or electrical components appear damaged.
{% endhint %}

***

### 2. Turn on the light

1. Plug the power supply's barrel connector into the light.
2. The LED should flash briefly and then turn off. This is expected.
3. Wait a moment, then press the power button to turn on the LED.

***

### 3. Test movement and power

With the LED on, slowly move the lamp through the full range of both axes. The LED should stay on the entire time.

If it cuts out, pay attention to whether it happens at a specific position or on one particular axis. Then see [Intermittent power dropouts](/open-task-light/troubleshooting/intermittent-power-dropouts.md).

***

### 4. If the light does not respond

Try a [clean power cycle](/open-task-light/troubleshooting.md#start-with-a-clean-power-cycle), then repeat the first power-on procedure.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Disconnect power immediately if you notice excessive heat, a burning smell, smoke, sparks, or other unexpected electrical behavior. Do not reconnect the power supply until the cause has been identified and corrected.
{% endhint %}

If the light still won't turn on, follow [No light after assembly](/open-task-light/troubleshooting/no-light-after-assembly.md) to check where power stops reaching the lamp.

Something still not working? See [Troubleshooting](/open-task-light/troubleshooting.md).
