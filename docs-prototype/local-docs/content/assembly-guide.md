# Assembly Guide

Once all of your parts are printed and cleaned up, you're ready to start putting the light together.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Keep the power supply disconnected throughout assembly. Connect power only when instructed in [Power-On & Test](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/power-on-and-test.md).
{% endhint %}

### Before you start

* Remove all support material and clean up the printed parts.
* Gather the required [tools and supplies](/open-task-light/start-here/tools-youll-need.md).
* Leave the small hardware in its labeled bags until you need it. It's much easier to keep track of that way.
* Decide whether you're building the freestanding or clamping base.

✨I put together an [OTL playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1LKR0dAHlcRFgwwC8PZeHU) with some of the music I listened to while working on the Open Task Light. Listen while you build if you want.✨

### Assembly order

1. [**Prepare the rails**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/prepare-the-rails.md) - Remove the power tracks and separate the rails.
2. [**Assemble the horizontal rail**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/horizontal-rail-assembly.md) - Add the LED, cooling parts, and electronics housing, then reinstall the horizontal power track.
3. [**Assemble your base**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/base-assembly.md) - Choose the freestanding or clamping version.
4. [**Assemble the carriage and counterweight**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/carriage-and-counterweight.md) - Build both parts and connect them with the belt.
5. [**Complete final assembly**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/final-assembly.md) - Join the rails and install the moving parts.
6. [**Power on and test**](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/power-on-and-test.md) - Give everything one last inspection, then connect power and test the light.
