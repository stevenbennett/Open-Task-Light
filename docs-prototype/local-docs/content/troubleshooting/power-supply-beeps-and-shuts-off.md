# Power supply beeps and shuts off

This means a short in the lamp has triggered the supplied power supply's protection mode. It stays off until it's unplugged from the wall and plugged back in. **Find and fix the short before reconnecting power.**

Unplug the power supply from the wall, disconnect the barrel connector, and disconnect any USB power before inspecting the wiring. Start with the custom barrel jack to Micro-Lock cable in the base. Kit cables are tested, but a stray wire strand or bit of solder can bridge the terminals inside the jack. This should be very rare, but it's worth checking on both kit and self-sourced cables.

The [freestanding](/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/freestanding-base-power-cable-assembly.md) and [clamping](/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/clamping-base-power-cable-assembly.md) cable guides show the connections. If you can't locate and resolve the short, [let me know](/open-task-light/troubleshooting.md#still-need-help) before plugging the lamp back in.
