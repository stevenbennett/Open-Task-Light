# Separate the rails

The rails are joined together to protect the heat pipe during shipping. Once the protective piece is removed the heat pipe is vulnerable to bending so I suggest doing this step once you're ready to move on to the [Horizontal Rail Assembly](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/horizontal-rail-assembly.md) step.

⚠️ We'll need the screws removed in this step later on so make sure you put them in their corresponding assembly bags after you remove them.

<figure>
<img src="/images/62d09f2b2c7d.jpg" alt="Two aluminum rails joined at one end by a black protective piece, with the copper heat pipe tip visible between them">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="87,32" label="78,12">Protective piece <note>Black printed part holding the two rails together for shipping. Two M5 screws fasten it, one into each rail.</note></li>
<li dot="83,39" label="88,58">Heat pipe <note>The copper tip poking out of the lower rail. Once the protective piece is off, this is easy to bend.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Use a 4 mm hex driver to remove the silver M5 socket-head screw.

<figure>
<img src="/images/e422aa3840ed.jpg" alt="A 4 mm hex driver seated in the silver M5 socket-head screw on the end of the protective piece">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="57,31" label="68,44" qty="1" match="silver M5 socket-head screw" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">Low profile M5 screw <note>The low-profile silver screw the driver is seated in. Keep it separate from the black M5: it goes in the Lighting assembly bag.</note></li>
<li dot="68,10" label="82,22" qty="Tool" link="Tools list: start-here/tools-youll-need">4 mm hex driver <note>Turn counter-clockwise to back the screw out.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Put the silver M5 socket-head screw in the Lighting assembly bag. This is a special low-profile socket head, and we'll use it later to attach the copper bracket to the rail.

<figure>
<img src="/images/6820357e2cad.jpg" alt="The Lighting assembly bag held in a hand with a 4 mm hex driver lying next to it">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="34,18" label="15,30" qty="1 bag" link="Kit contents (BOM): self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">Lighting assembly bag <note>The bag labelled LIGHTING. The silver low-profile M5 screw goes in here with the LED parts.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Carefully slide the rail with the heatpipe out of the protective cover.

<figure><img src="/images/ef5ef2919b9c.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Going forward we'll refer to the rail with the heat pipe as the horizontal rail, the other rail is the vertical rail.

<figure>
<img src="/images/89bbd9033311.jpg" alt="The horizontal rail on its own with the copper heat pipe tip sticking out of one end">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="40,53" label="30,35" link="Rail prep: self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/v-slot-rail">Horizontal rail <note>The rail with the heat pipe. Handle it by the rail, not the pipe.</note></li>
<li dot="77,17" label="85,30">Heat pipe <note>Exposed copper tip. Don't lean on it or let it hang off the table edge.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Remove the M5 screw from the vertical rail.

<figure>
<img src="/images/d71b41aa1d3f.jpg" alt="A 4 mm hex driver removing the M5 screw that holds the protective piece to the vertical rail">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="45,51" label="25,40" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5 screw <note>The second screw, on the vertical rail side of the protective piece. Goes in the Spare Parts + Tools bag.</note></li>
<li dot="70,20" label="60,8">Vertical rail <note>The rail without the heat pipe.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Place the M5 screw in the Spare Parts + Tools bag.

<figure>
<img src="/images/0faa316c45f8.jpg" alt="An M5 screw held above the open Spare Parts + Tools bag">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="43,32" label="20,22" qty="1 bag" link="Kit contents (BOM): self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">Spare Parts + Tools bag <note>The bag labelled SPARE PARTS + TOOLS. Not the Lighting bag.</note></li>
<li dot="52,23" label="88,12" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5 screw <note>The black screw from the vertical rail. It's a spare from here on.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Peel the protective film from the horizontal and vertical rails.

<figure><img src="/images/c13d8b11a02e.jpg" alt=""></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You may see some residue on your rails from the factory protective film. You can use alcohol to clean this up if you'd like. Goo Gone works really well too. This seems to be more common on the black rails for some reason.
{% endhint %}

Your rails are ready for assembly! Remember to be extra careful with heat pipe. Ideally, move directly to [Horizontal Rail Assembly](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/horizontal-rail-assembly.md)
