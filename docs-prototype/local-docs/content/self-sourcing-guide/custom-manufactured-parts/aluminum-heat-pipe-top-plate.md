This part is the top half of the sandwich that secures the heat pipe to the LED. It can be laser cut from aluminum, or copper if you prefer, and has two M3 tapped holes. You can use a service like [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com/) or [OSH Cut](https://oshcut.com/) to make this part.

## Manufacturing specifications

| Specification        | Requirement                                                                                           |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Material             | 6061-T6 aluminum                                                                                      |
| Thickness            | 0.063 in (1.6 mm)                                                                                     |
| Cutting process      | Laser cut                                                                                             |
| Secondary operations | Tap the two smaller holes M3 x 0.5 after cutting. This can be done by the cutting service or by hand. |
