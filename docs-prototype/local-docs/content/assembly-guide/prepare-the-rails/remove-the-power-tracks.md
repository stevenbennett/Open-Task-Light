# Remove the power tracks

Start by removing the power tracks from the rails.

⚠️ **Warning:** The power connectors on the power tracks are fragile. I know it's tempting, but please never move the power tracks using this connector.

<figure>
<img src="/images/f522f744b6c5.jpg" alt="The two rails still joined together, each with a brass power track installed and the black protective piece on the right end">

Use the grub screw and a 1.5 mm hex driver to slide the power track until the end is exposed.

<figure>
<img src="/images/6d134de08ee6.jpg" alt="A 1.5 mm hex driver seated in the grub screw on a power track, sliding the track toward the end of the rail">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="51,48" label="57,18" match="grub screw">Power track set screw <note>Seat the driver here and push along the rail until the end of the track is exposed.</note></li>
<li dot="55,56" label="84,45" qty="Tool" link="Tools list: start-here/tools-youll-need">1.5 mm hex driver <note>Smallest driver in the kit.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/9b971ca75f3a.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Once the end is exposed, grab it from the sides and pull the power track all the way out.

<figure><img src="/images/9659edd709e9.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Repeat for the second power track. Store these in a safe place for later.
