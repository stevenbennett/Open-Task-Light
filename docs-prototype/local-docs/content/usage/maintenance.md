# Maintenance

Not much maintenance is required on the Open Task Light. Check it periodically, especially if dust begins to collect.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Disconnect power before cleaning. Do not spray the light, rinse any component, or allow liquid to enter the electronics, power tracks, or power supply. The Open Task Light is not water-resistant.
{% endhint %}

#### **Clean the power tracks**

Unplug the light and remove dust from the power tracks with a clean, dry, lint-free cloth. For stubborn residue, lightly dampen the cloth with isopropyl alcohol—never apply liquid directly to the light. Allow the tracks to dry completely before reconnecting power. Keeping the tracks clean helps the axes move smoothly and quietly and supports reliable electrical contact.

#### Check carriage tightness

The wheels hold the carriage close enough to the power tracks for the spring-loaded pins to make contact. If the carriage is loose against either rail, power may not reach the horizontal rail, or the light may cut out as you move it.

There are two adjustable wheels: one for the vertical rail and one for the horizontal rail. Each uses an **eccentric nut**, the silver hex-shaped spacer between the wheel and the printed carriage. Turning this spacer moves the wheel closer to or farther from the rail.

You'll need the **OTL wrench or an 8 mm wrench**. If a wheel's mounting screw is loose, you'll also need a **3 mm hex driver**.

1. **Disconnect power.** Unplug the barrel connector and disconnect any USB cable from its power source. The USB cable can stay plugged into the QT Py. Leave the horizontal arm installed and support it while checking the fit.
2. **Check the wheel fasteners.** The screws and outer locknuts holding the wheels should be secure. If one is loose, tighten it by hand with the 3 mm hex driver and wrench as shown in [Final Assembly](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/final-assembly.md#attach-the-carriage). You shouldn't need extra leverage.
3. **Check both rails for play.** Hold the vertical rail steady and gently wiggle the carriage. Then hold the carriage and gently check the horizontal arm for play against its wheels.
4. **Adjust the loose side.** Put the wrench on the eccentric spacer between the wheel and carriage, and turn it a little at a time. This adjusts the fit against the rail; tightening the outer locknut isn't the same adjustment. Depending on its starting position, either direction may bring the wheel closer to the rail.
5. **Check movement after each small adjustment.** Stop when the play is gone, then move the carriage or arm along the rail. It should feel smooth, with very little resistance. If it starts to bind, turn the eccentric back a little. Repeat for the other rail if needed.


Reconnect power and check the light again. If you're following the [troubleshooting guide](/open-task-light/troubleshooting/no-light-after-assembly.md#check-the-horizontal-power-track), repeat the horizontal power-track measurement. Once the light turns on, move it through the full range of both axes and make sure it stays on.

If both rails move smoothly without play but power still isn't getting through, return to the [power checks](/open-task-light/troubleshooting/no-light-after-assembly.md#check-the-horizontal-power-track). Don't keep tightening the wheels to try to force electrical contact.
