### Install the static carriage wheels

The fully assembled carriage uses six wheels but at this stage we'll only install four. The last two are installed during final assembly when you join the rails.

<figure>
<img src="/images/78cf4e37e393.jpg" alt="Carriage body with six wheels, six M5x27 screws, aluminum spacers, eccentric nuts and lock nuts laid out">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="40,20" label="72,14" qty="1" match="carriage" link="Print guide: printing-guide/carriage/carriage-body">Carriage Body <note>Four small counterbored holes take the static wheels now; the two larger counterbores stay empty.</note></li>
<li dot="58,53" label="78,58" qty="6 (4 now)" match="wheels" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">Wheel <note>Install four now. The last two go on during final assembly.</note></li>
<li dot="34,50" label="22,44" qty="6 (4 now)" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5x27 mm screw <note>Low-profile head. Four are used now, two stay in the bag for final assembly.</note></li>
<li dot="41,65" label="52,80" qty="4" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5x6 mm aluminum spacer <note>The round silver spacers. One goes on each static wheel screw.</note></li>
<li dot="43.5,59" label="52,43" qty="2" match="eccentric spacers" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials">M5 eccentric spacer <note>The hex silver ones. Not used on this page; they go in the two larger counterbores during final assembly.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
There are two types of holes on the carriage: four holes with counterbores just large enough for the screw heads, and two holes with larger counterbores to accommodate the eccentric spacers. We're going to install screws in the first four holes.

Locate the two counterbored holes on each side and insert a M5x27 mm screw from one side and a M5x6 mm aluminum spacer on the other.

<figure>
<img src="/images/b0bc4c17bc29.jpg" alt="Carriage body held in both hands with an M5x27 screw pushed through from the far side and an aluminum spacer sitting on the shank on the near side">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="38,57" label="14,42">M5x6 mm aluminum spacer <note>Goes on the side opposite the screw head, over the shank. The wheel then goes on after it.</note></li>
<li dot="41.5,59.5" label="32,22">M5x27 mm screw <note>Inserted from the far side; only its tip shows here. Use one of the four small-counterbore holes, not the two larger ones.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Before you add the wheels, take a look inside to make sure the spacer inside isn't blocking the screw path. If it is, use a screwdriver to nudge it out of the way.

Add a wheel and a lock nut to each screw. Tighten the wheels down using a 3 mm hex driver together with either the small end of the OTL wrench or an 8 mm wrench.

<figure>
<img src="/images/ebe9356def78.jpg" alt="Tightening a wheel with the 3 mm hex driver on the screw head and the small end of the OTL wrench holding the lock nut">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="42,60" label="78,80" link="Tools list: start-here/tools-youll-need">OTL wrench <note>Its small end holds the lock nut while you drive the screw. An 8 mm wrench works too.</note></li>
<li dot="62,41" label="76,18">3 mm hex driver <note>Drives the screw head from the far side.</note></li>
<li dot="36,56" label="10,44">Lock nut <note>Sits in the small end of the wrench.</note></li>
<li dot="59,50" label="72,46">Wheel <note>Wheel goes on the screw first, then the lock nut.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Repeat the wheel installation on the opposite side of the carriage in the positions shown below.

<figure>
<img src="/images/02e023db89a8.jpg" alt="Carriage with four static wheels installed, seen from one side, with the two larger counterbored holes still empty">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="59,29" label="74,22" qty="2 this side" match="wheel">Wheel <note>The lock nut faces you on the two wheels mounted from this side.</note></li>
<li dot="57.5,55.5" label="78,38" qty="2 far side" match="wheel">Wheel <note>Mounted from the other side; only its screw head shows here. The two larger counterbored holes stay empty.</note></li>
<li dot="30.5,58" label="14,60" qty="2 far side" match="wheel">Wheel <note>The other far-side wheel, peeking out behind the arm.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/2428146253c1.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Return the remaining pairs of wheels, M5x27 mm screws, eccentric nuts, and lock nuts to the carriage bag. You'll need them for the final assembly step.
