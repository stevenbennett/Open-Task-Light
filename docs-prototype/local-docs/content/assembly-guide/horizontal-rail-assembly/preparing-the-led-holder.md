### Insert wires into the LED holder

On the LED holder, locate the wire inlet labeled C+.

<figure>
<img src="/images/625bb892512c.jpg" alt="Close-up of the underside of the LED holder showing the C+ wire inlet slot beside the square LED window">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="41.5,45.5" label="66,32" match="wire inlet labeled C+">C+ wire inlet <note>The narrow slot beside the square window, marked C+ in the moulding. The positive (red-marked) wire slides in here, under the spring terminal.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Find the wire with the red marking on the end, this is the positive wire.

<figure>
<img src="/images/3debef30a038.jpg" alt="Two bare wire ends held up, the right one with a red marking near its tip">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52.3,46" label="70,26" match="red marking">Positive wire (red marking) <note>This one goes into the C+ inlet. Hold it close to the bare tip when inserting so it doesn't bend; the unmarked wire goes into C- afterwards.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Hold the positive wire as close as possible to the exposed wire and insert it into the C+ wire inlet of the LED holder. Try to angle the wire slightly downward so it can more easily slide under the terminal.

{% hint style="info" %}
This is one of the trickier steps in the assembly process. It may take a couple of tries, and the wire might get bent during your attempts. If this happens, straighten the wire and try again. You'll get it eventually!
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="/images/088965de5aad.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/fe3b37703e75.jpg" alt=""></figure>

After inserting the wire, give it a little tug. If it's inserted correctly the wire shouldn't pull out.

<figure><img src="/images/7952be401808.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Insert the unmarked wire (GND) in the C- inlet. Note the orientation of the cable in the image below; we want the connector to sit above the top of the LED holder like this:

<img src="/images/0fd07661783b.jpg" alt="The LED holder held up with both wires inserted and the cable looping above it to the connector">
