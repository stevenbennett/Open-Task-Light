# Safety

The Open Task Light is a kit that requires assembly. Read and follow the assembly instructions before use.

* Disconnect the power supply before assembling, adjusting, or servicing electrical components.
* Use only a 24 V DC power supply that meets the specifications listed in this guide. Do not connect the light directly to mains power.
* Use only in a dry, indoor environment. The light is not water-resistant. Do not expose the light, electronics, power tracks, or power supply to water, splashes, condensation, or other moisture.
* Avoid dusty environments. Dust can collect on the exposed power tracks and moving components, which may impair movement or cause intermittent electrical contact.
* Do not use the light if wiring, connectors, circuit boards, the power supply, or other electrical components are damaged.
* The LED is very bright. Avoid looking directly into the illuminated LED, particularly at high brightness.
* The LED assembly and nearby components may become warm during operation. Allow them to cool before servicing.
* Keep fingers clear of wheels, joints, and moving components while adjusting the light.
* Keep small parts away from children.

Modifying the electrical, mechanical, or software design may introduce hazards not covered by these instructions. Take appropriate precautions when making modifications.
