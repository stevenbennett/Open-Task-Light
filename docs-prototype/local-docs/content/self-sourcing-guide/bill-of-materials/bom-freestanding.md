# Freestanding OTL BOM

Parts and materials required for the freestanding Open Task Light build.

Everything you need to source and build the freestanding version of the Open Task Light.

Parts you print yourself are covered by the [main Printing Guide](/open-task-light/printing-guide.md). Parts normally supplied with a kit are covered by [Additional Printed Parts](/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/additional-printed-parts.md).

Parts that need additional preparation link to their guides in the [Custom Manufactured Parts](/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/custom-manufactured-parts.md) or [Manual Part Preparation](/open-task-light/self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation.md) sections.
