# Joining the carriage and the counterweight

### Gather the following

* Assembled **Carriage** (from [Assembling the carriage](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/carriage-and-counterweight/assembling-the-carriage.md))
* Assembled **Counterweight** (from [Assembling the counterweight](/open-task-light/assembly-guide/carriage-and-counterweight/assembling-the-counterweight.md))
* **`Power Link`** parts bag
* Printed parts
  * [Carriage Clip](/open-task-light/printing-guide/carriage/carriage-clip.md)

Tools:

* **1.3 mm hex driver**

### Install the power link

Insert the power link in the carriage (it's symmetrical so it can go in either way).

{% hint style="danger" %}
The power link should drop into the slot easily. **If you're having trouble, don't force it.** Check the printer with the [Hardware Fit Tester](/open-task-light/printing-guide/before-you-start/hardware-fit-tester.md), [correct the filament flow](/open-task-light/printing-guide/before-you-start/filament-flow-calibration.md) if needed, and reprint the carriage. Check out the [Carriage Body](/open-task-light/printing-guide/carriage/carriage-body.md) printing guide for more information.
{% endhint %}

### Secure the belt to the carriage

Ensure the belt isn't twisted, then insert it, toothside down through the back of the carriage.

<figure>
<img src="/images/70ea53f52138.jpg" alt="Hands feeding the end of the belt, teeth down, into the slot on the back of the carriage">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="46,43" label="24,26" link="Part prep: self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/gt2-timing-belt">Belt <note>Toothside down: the teeth face into the carriage so they can mesh with the clip. It goes in through the slot on the back face, beside the power link.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Push it through the carriage by 2-3 inches.

<figure><img src="/images/f5f2862b9156.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Align the end of the belt with the teeth of the carriage clip, holding them together with your fingers.

<figure>
<img src="/images/c10ba7e7d13c.jpg" alt="Fingers holding the end of the belt against the toothed side of the carriage clip, in front of the carriage">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="45.5,44" label="30,8" link="Print guide: printing-guide/carriage/carriage-clip">Carriage Clip <note>Its teeth face the belt. Mesh the belt end with the clip teeth, end flush with the end of the clip, and hold the two together while you insert the clip.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/01c0deb08c09.jpg" alt="Close-up of fingers holding the end of the belt against the toothed side of the carriage clip"></figure>

While holding the belt in place, insert the carriage clip into the carriage.

Start by inserting the bottom of the clip.

<figure><img src="/images/60b9a7949f2d.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Then push the top of the clip into place. This method should push the excess belt out the back of the carriage allowing the clip to lie flat.

<figure><img src="/images/6b3e372354ab.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/55e5955608b4.jpg" alt=""></figure>

### Attach the carriage clip

Use the three M2x6 mm flathead screws found in the "Power Link" bag to secure the clip in place. Finger tighten.
