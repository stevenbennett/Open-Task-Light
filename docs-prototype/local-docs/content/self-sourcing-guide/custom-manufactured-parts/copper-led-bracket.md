This LED bracket serves two roles: it is the bottom half of the heat pipe sandwich and the mounting plate for the LED. For maximum thermal conductivity, make this part from copper. The part requires a 90-degree bend, and I recommend having the manufacturer do this. The bend line is included in the drawing. You can use a service like [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com/) or [OSH Cut](https://oshcut.com/) to make this part.

## Manufacturing specifications

| Specification   | Requirement                                   |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Material        | C110 copper                                   |
| Thickness       | 0.063 in (1.6 mm)                             |
| Cutting process | Laser cut                                     |
| Forming         | 90-degree bend; bend line included in drawing |
