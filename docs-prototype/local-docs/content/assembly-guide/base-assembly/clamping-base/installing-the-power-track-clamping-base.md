# Installing the power track (clamping base)

### Gather the following

* **Assembled clamping base** (from previous step)
* **Power track** (1x)
* **M3 set screw** (threaded into the power track before shipping)
* Printed Parts
  * [Power Track Support](/open-task-light/printing-guide/rail-attachments/power-track-support.md)(2x)
  * [Power Track Offset Tool](/open-task-light/printing-guide/tools/power-track-offset-tool.md)

Tools:

* **1.5 mm hex driver**

***

### Insert the power track

This empty channel is where the power track will go. If we imagine that the printed rail bottom occupies the rail's 9 o'clock channel, the power track will occupy the 6 o'clock channel.

<figure>
<img src="/images/b0cfec333225.jpg" alt="Finger pointing at the empty 6 o'clock channel of the vertical rail above the clamping base, with the power cable hanging beside it">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50,30" label="24,30" match="6 o'clock channel">6 o'clock channel <note>The empty channel facing the camera. The power track slides down this one.</note></li>
<li dot="46,27" label="24,12" match="printed rail bottom" link="Print guide: printing-guide/clamping-base/rail-bottom">Rail bottom <note>The dark strip is the printed rail bottom's tab, sitting in the 9 o'clock channel. Use it to orient yourself.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
At the top of the rail, insert the power track (connecter end first) into the 6 o'clock rail channel.

⚠️ Friendly reminder: the cable connectors on the power track are fragile. Don't use these to move the board around; always use the board edges.

<figure>
<img src="/images/6e89f6784336.jpg" alt="Hand lowering the power track PCB into the top of the vertical rail, connector end first">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50,15" label="25,15" qty="1" link="Part page: self-sourcing-guide/custom-manufactured-parts/power-track">Power track <note>Hold it by the board edges, never by the connectors.</note></li>
<li dot="50,45" label="25,52" qty="Goes in first" match="connecter end first">Connector end <note>The fragile cable connectors are on this end. It leads into the rail.</note></li>
<li dot="51,33" label="72,45" match="6 o'clock rail channel">6 o'clock channel <note>The empty channel opposite the printed rail bottom.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Once the power track is submerged in the rail by a few inches, insert your first power track spacer with the printbed side of the spacer touching the smooth back of the power track PCB.

<figure>
<img src="/images/c4df4b5c85a2.jpg" alt="First printed power track spacer being pressed onto the back of the power track at the top of the rail">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="45,36" label="20,32" qty="1st of 2" match="power track spacer" link="Print guide: printing-guide/rail-attachments/power-track-support">Power track support <note>The printed spacer (the Power Track Support print). The printbed (flat) side faces the power track.</note></li>
<li dot="44,14" label="20,12">Smooth back of the power track PCB <note>Insert the spacer against this side, not the side with the copper tracks.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Slide the spacer and the power track down until you have room to insert the second printed spacer. Push the spacer as far down as possible before sliding the power track all the way down.

<figure><img src="/images/9f7f05ad1cf8.jpg" alt=""></figure>

### Set the power track height

At the base of the lamp, place the power track offset tool (clamping base version) under the power track to set the height.

<figure>
<img src="/images/34b05f661c35.jpg" alt="Hand holding the clamping base version of the power track offset tool under the power track just above the base body">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52,40" label="72,18" qty="1 printed" link="Print guide: printing-guide/tools/power-track-offset-tool">Power track offset tool <note>Clamping base version. Sits between the base body and the bottom of the power track to set its height.</note></li>
<li dot="50,15" label="25,15">Power track <note>Slide it down until it rests on the tool, then tighten the set screw.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
### Secure and connect the power track

Use a 1.5 mm hex driver to secure the power track in place. Finger tighten.

<figure>
<img src="/images/360f118a8f1b.jpg" alt="1.5 mm hex driver tightening the M3 set screw on the power track through the rail channel">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="30,47" label="32,68" qty="Tool" link="Tools list: start-here/tools-youll-need">1.5 mm hex driver <note>Its tip is in the M3 set screw that comes threaded into the top of the power track. Finger tighten only; over-tightening can crack the PCB.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Remove the power track offset tool and plug the power cable into the power track.

<figure>

</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/bf18f16a76a6.jpg" alt="Clamping base power cable plugged into the connector at the bottom of the power track, with the offset tool removed"></figure>
</div>
You're done with the vertical rail assembly!
