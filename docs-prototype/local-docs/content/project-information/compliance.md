# Compliance

The Open Task Light has been tested for compliance as an unintentional radiator under FCC Part 15 and was found to comply with the applicable limits for a Class B digital device.

These limits are intended to provide reasonable protection against harmful interference in a residential installation.

Operation is subject to the condition that this device does not cause harmful interference and accepts any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

Modifications to the electronics or use outside the tested configuration may affect compliance.
