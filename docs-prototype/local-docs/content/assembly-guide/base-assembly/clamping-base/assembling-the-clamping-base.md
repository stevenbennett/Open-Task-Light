# Assembling the clamping base

### Gather the following

* **`Clamping Base`** parts bag
* Printed Parts
  * [Body](/open-task-light/printing-guide/clamping-base/body.md)
  * [Rail Bottom](/open-task-light/printing-guide/clamping-base/rail-bottom.md)
  * [Power Plug Clip](/open-task-light/printing-guide/clamping-base/power-plug-clip.md)
* **Vertical rail**
* **Power brick**

Tools:

* **3 mm hex driver**

### Prepare the clamping base body

#### Insert the spacer

Find the 3 mm tall M5 space in the clamping base bag and drop it into the hole in the printed base body.

<figure>
<img src="/images/0df96f9e891b.jpg" alt="Fingers holding the small M5 spacer next to the printed base body, ready to drop it in the top hole">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="50,47" label="50,22" qty="1" match="3 mm tall M5 space" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">3 mm M5 spacer <note>The thin steel ring from the Clamping Base bag. Drop it in flat.</note></li>
<li dot="34,40" label="20,22">Hole in the printed base body <note>The spacer sits at the bottom of this hole; the sleeve bearing goes in on top of it.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/1ffe1abd8c2d.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Use a broad-tipped tool to press the spacer into place.

<figure><img src="/images/d60863913db5.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/680d16cd9bd1.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Ensure the spacer sits flat at the bottom of the base.

<figure><img src="/images/04439fb2af27.jpg" alt=""></figure>

#### Insert the sleeve bearing

Take the sleeve bearing from the bag.

<figure>
<img src="/images/10c61033f043.jpg" alt="Fingers holding the bronze sleeve bearing beside the printed base body">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49,45" label="50,12" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">Sleeve bearing <note>Bronze sleeve. Press it into the top hole until it is flush with the top of the body.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Insert it into the base body and press it into place.

<figure><img src="/images/0eea4c1f4b7e.jpg" alt=""></figure>

You make need to flip the base upside-down and press it down on your work surface to force the bearing into place.

<figure><img src="/images/e091248de4b2.jpg" alt=""></figure>

<figure><img src="/images/8d12c77505ca.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Make sure the bearing is flush with the top surface of the base.

<figure><img src="/images/e94b184c698f.jpg" alt=""></figure>

#### Add the ball bearing

Find the 5.5 mm ball bearing in the kit bag and drop it into the base so that it sits inside of the M5 spacer you inserted earlier.

<figure>
<img src="/images/a7d8f3c1ae5a.jpg" alt="Fingers holding the 5.5 mm ball bearing over the sleeve bearing in the top of the base body">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49,47" label="58,14" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">5.5 mm ball bearing <note>Small steel ball from the kit bag. Drop it through the sleeve bearing; it rests inside the 3 mm M5 spacer at the bottom of the hole.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/927eb7604615.jpg" alt=""></figure>

### Assemble the vertical rail

Gather the following parts to attach the rail to the base.

<figure>
<img src="/images/373cf347d9a6.jpg" alt="Vertical rail, prepared base body, M5x50 mm screw, steel spacer and printed rail bottom laid out on a table">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="60,43" label="70,25" qty="1" match="rail">Vertical rail <note>The marked end (extra-long tapped hole) is the bottom.</note></li>
<li dot="32,52" label="20,76" qty="1" match="base" link="Print guide: printing-guide/clamping-base/body">Base body <note>With the spacer, sleeve bearing and ball bearing already installed.</note></li>
<li dot="45,58" label="38,86" qty="1" match="long screw" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">M5x50 mm socket head screw <note>The long screw. Passes through the steel spacer (the short steel tube beside it) into the end of the rail. The printed rail bottom is on the right.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Locate the marked end of your vertical rail. This end is tapped extra long to accommodate the long screw used in the clamping base, so this will be the "bottom" of your vertical rail.

<figure>
<img src="/images/55fdd6be7e8c.jpg" alt="Hands holding the marked end of the vertical rail next to the base body">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="49,46" label="65,28" match="marked end">Marked end of the rail (bottom) <note>This end is tapped extra long for the M5x50 mm screw. The rail bottom goes in here; the other end will be the top of the lamp.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Insert the rail bottom into the end of the rail.

<figure><img src="/images/9a6f1ee7619c.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Insert the steel spacer into the rail bottom.

<figure>
<img src="/images/96c248ef1a8a.jpg" alt="Steel spacer being pushed into the printed rail bottom on the end of the vertical rail">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52,50" label="66,28" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">Steel spacer <note>Slides into the hole in the rail bottom.</note></li>
<li dot="45,44" label="45,74" link="Print guide: printing-guide/clamping-base/rail-bottom">Rail bottom <note>Already inserted into the marked end of the rail.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Secure the spacer to the rail using the M5x50 mm socket head screw.

<figure>
<img src="/images/f6d1ef68ce9b.jpg" alt="M5x50 mm socket head screw being started into the steel spacer at the end of the rail">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="52,52" label="62,30" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">M5x50 mm socket head screw <note>3 mm hex. Threads through the spacer into the rail's long tapped hole.</note></li>
<li dot="45,47" label="42,80" match="spacer">Steel spacer <note>The screw clamps this to the end of the rail.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Fully tighten the screw using a 3 mm hex tool like an Allen key where you can generate some leverage.

<figure><img src="/images/d9cf34a6dc93.jpg" alt=""></figure>

### Prepare the c-clamp

Gather the clamping base power cable, the c-clamp, and the power plug clip.

<figure>
<img src="/images/5ed4335db00c.jpg" alt="Clamping base power cable, c-clamp and printed power plug clip laid out on a table">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="34,48" label="22,24" qty="1" link="How it is made: self-sourcing-guide/manual-part-preparation/clamping-base-power-cable-assembly">Clamping base power cable <note>The barrel jack end (with the metal rim) goes into the clip; the small connector end plugs into the power track.</note></li>
<li dot="53,45" label="50,22" qty="1" link="BOM: self-sourcing-guide/bill-of-materials/bom-clamping">C-clamp <note>Comes in the Clamping Base bag.</note></li>
<li dot="65,50" label="80,38" qty="1 printed" link="Print guide: printing-guide/clamping-base/power-plug-clip">Power plug clip <note>Hooks around the clamp frame and holds the cable plug.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Start by opening the c-clamp to roughly the height of the surface you plan to clamp your Open Task Light to.

<div>
<figure><img src="/images/0cfc5d0d52d7.jpg" alt=""></figure>
<figure><img src="/images/38138b88912f.jpg" alt=""></figure>
</div>

The power plug clip has a large hook (left) and a small hook (right), and a spot for the power cable plug with a slot in it. The open end of the slot should be facing up when you install it (away from the threaded clamp rod).

<figure>
<img src="/images/fbacb2029367.jpg" alt="Close-up of the power plug clip showing the large hook on the left, small hook on the right and the slotted plug holder in the middle">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="47,44" label="25,15" match="large hook">Large hook (left) <note>Goes around the clamp frame first.</note></li>
<li dot="55,44" label="66,14" match="small hook">Small hook (right) <note>Snaps into place last.</note></li>
<li dot="51,48" label="82,32" match="open end of the slot">Open end of the slot (faces up) <note>The plug drops into this round holder. Keep the slot opening pointing away from the threaded clamp rod.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
Pick the side you want your clip to be on, and hook the large hook around the edge on the opposing side (in this photo, the clip will end up on the right side of the clamp (from the camera's perspective)).

<figure><img src="/images/7551cab7e99a.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Rotate the clip towards your desired side.

<figure><img src="/images/75a4b7f8f4bc.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Snap the small hook into place.

<figure><img src="/images/d4d75f2d185f.jpg" alt=""></figure>

Insert the power cable into the clip so that the metal rim is sticking out the bottom.

<div>
<figure>
<img src="/images/8cc9fef868b8.jpg" alt="Hand holding the power cable plug, metal rim first, next to the power plug clip snapped onto the c-clamp">
<ol class="callouts">
<li dot="43,47" label="30,10" match="metal rim">Metal rim <note>The metal rim of the barrel jack. Once inserted it should stick out below the clip, not sit inside it.</note></li>
<li dot="52,46" label="72,32" match="clip" link="Print guide: printing-guide/clamping-base/power-plug-clip">Power plug clip <note>Snapped onto the c-clamp frame with the slot facing up. The plug drops in from the top.</note></li>
</ol>
</figure>
<figure><img src="/images/50b14c5b1c34.jpg" alt=""></figure>
<figure><img src="/images/57ec3df8cd5c.jpg" alt="Power cable seated in the clip on the c-clamp with the metal rim of the plug sticking out the bottom"></figure>
</div>
